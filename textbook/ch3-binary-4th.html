Time as the 4th Dimension

In classical physics, time is the 4th dimension — continuous and directional. But in the framework here, we treat the 4th dimension as binary: a coin flip. This maps to quantum mechanics, where a particle exists in superposition until observed.

The Coin as d2

A coin is a degenerate polyhedron — a d2. Before it lands, both states (heads/tails) are equally valid. Gravity (observation) collapses the superposition. In a schema, this is a nullable boolean that resolves to TRUE or FALSE upon a triggering event.
