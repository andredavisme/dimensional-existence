Chapter 1: What Is a Dimension?

← Textbook

Definition

A dimension is an independent axis of existence — a direction in which something can vary without affecting any other axis. In our physical world, we experience three spatial dimensions (x, y, z) plus time.

Finite vs. Extensive Dimensions

Some dimensions are finite — they have a bounded, countable set of states (like a die face). Others are extensive — they span a continuous or unbounded range (like position in space).

  • A coin represents a finite binary dimension: exactly 2 states.
  • A d6 die represents a finite hexagonal dimension: exactly 6 states.
  • A ruler represents an extensive dimension: infinite real-valued states.
