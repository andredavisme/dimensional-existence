The Gravity Analogy

Gravity is the universal constraint of the physical world — it forces a rolled die to settle on exactly one face. In a database, CHECK constraints, NOT NULL, UNIQUE, and foreign keys are the gravity: they prevent invalid states from persisting.

  • 📖 Supabase: Row Level Security

Cascade as Physical Law

A CASCADE rule on a foreign key is the schema equivalent of a physical law: when a parent dimension changes state, all child dimensions update in concert. This models how entangled particles or gravitationally bound systems behave.
